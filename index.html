Zenith Gold Reference

Everything for Pokémon Zenith Gold in one place: every trainer's team, the damage calculator with all trainer sets preloaded, and a Pokédex with every learnset, ability, innate and wild encounter.

Trainer SheetEvery fight, split by split, with items, abilities, innates and notes. Damage CalcPokémon Null's calc with this game's data: every trainer set preloaded, import your team from mGBA. Using the exporter

  1. Download soulgold_export.lua above and keep it anywhere.
  2. In mGBA (0.10 or newer) with the game running: Tools → Scripting…, then File → Load script….
  3. Your party and PC boxes appear in the SoulGold sets tab and in soulgold_sets.txt next to the script, updating as you play.
  4. In the calculator, paste the text into its import box (under your Pokémon) and press Import.
